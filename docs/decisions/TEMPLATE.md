# NNNN – The decision in a few words

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN
- **In place since:** version and date, once released
- **Sources:** where the reasons are written down — docs, code comments,
  issues, changelog entries

## Context

What makes a decision necessary, and which constraints apply? Write it for
someone who was not part of the discussion.

## Decision

What was decided, in a few sentences. Name the files or modules that carry it.

## Alternatives considered

The strong alternatives and why they were not taken, a few lines each. "None
recorded" is an acceptable answer; a reason reconstructed after the fact is not.

## Consequences

What follows for stored data, teachers, the plans the algorithm produces,
operators and later changes — including the uncomfortable parts.
